# Platform specifications

Data version `2026.10.13`, generated from `data/platform-specs.json`. Do not edit this file by hand.

Read this before writing for a platform. Every fact carries a kind, a verification date and a source type. Only `hard` and `policy` facts may be stated to the user as platform rules. `algorithm` facts are signals, and `heuristic` facts are craft advice. If the preflight report lists a platform as stale, say so briefly and offer a refresh (see `spec-refresh.md`).

Ranking signals are not recipes. Published weights multiply predicted probabilities, so they do not mean "one reply equals N likes". Never promise reach.

## Retired claims

These circulate widely, including in earlier versions of this skill. They are wrong or unsupported. Do not repeat them.

- External links in X posts are suppressed 50 to 90 percent; put the link in a reply. X published its ranking code in 2026. It has no link penalty and X's head of product said links do not deboost posts.
- X weights: replies 13.5x, retweets 20x, bookmarks 10x, likes 1x, author reply 150x. Official weights: like 0.6, reply 5.0, repost 1.0, quote 5.0. Bookmarks carry no weight. Weights multiply predicted probabilities, not raw counts.
- YouTube Shorts are limited to 60 seconds. Shorts allow up to 3 minutes since 2024-10-15.
- YouTube Shorts must be 9:16 vertical only. Square or vertical is accepted.
- TikTok videos are 15 seconds to 10 minutes. In-app uploads allow up to 60 minutes; recording up to 10 minutes.
- Threads allows one link per post and 10 images. The API allows 5 unique links per post and carousels of up to 20 items.
- Bluesky allows 4 images and has limited video. 10 images via the gallery embed and 10 minute video as of mid 2026.
- Bluesky has 25 million users. 45 million reported in June 2026.
- Instagram: max 20 percent text on a slide; first slide drives 80 percent of results. The text rule is a legacy Facebook ads rule and the 80 percent figure has no source.
- LinkedIn: external links cut reach by 60 percent; 5+ word comments and dwell time carry fixed weights. LinkedIn denies intentionally limiting link posts and says published signal weights are myths.
- LinkedIn carousels are PDF only. Document posts accept PDF, PPT, PPTX, DOC and DOCX up to 100 MB and 300 pages.
- AI-generated TikTok videos are deprioritised. TikTok says labelled AI content stays eligible for For You.
- Newsletter button CTAs lift CTR 45 percent; question CTAs double replies; first-person CTAs add 20 percent. No primary source for any of these figures.
